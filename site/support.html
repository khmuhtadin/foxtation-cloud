Questions

Is Foxtation free?

Yes. The app is free and open source, and dictation on your Mac is unlimited. Foxtation Pro is an optional subscription for cloud transcription and cleanup.

What does Foxtation Pro add?

Nothing to download: it transcribes in the cloud and is much more accurate than on-device. It also fixes misheard words, removes filler words, applies your self-corrections and writes in the tone each app calls for.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 or later.

Which languages does it understand?

23 languages, including English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese and Indonesian. Pick the ones you speak in Settings › General, and you can mix two in one sentence.

Does my voice leave my Mac?

Not with the free on-device engines. With Foxtation Pro's Cloud engine, your speech is sent to be transcribed and isn't stored. The Privacy Policy has the details.

How do I activate Pro?

Open Foxtation, go to Settings › License, paste the license key from your purchase email and press Activate.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel anytime in the customer portal at polar.sh/khaisa-studios/portal. For a refund within 14 days of a payment, email hello@khaisa.studio.

macOS says the app can't be opened. What do I do?

Foxtation isn't notarized yet. Open it once, then approve it in System Settings › Privacy & Security, or run: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Foxtation.app
