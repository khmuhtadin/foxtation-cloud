Up and running in a minute.

macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon. Not notarized yet, so macOS asks you to approve it once.

Download the .dmg
  1. 1

    Drag it into Applications, or use Homebrew:

    $ brew install --cask khmuhtadin/tap/foxtation
  2. 2

    Open it once and approve it in System Settings › Privacy & Security, or run:

    $ xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Foxtation.app
  3. 3

    Free downloads a 1.6 GB model once. Pro just needs your license.
