Speak Indonesian, English, or both in one sentence. Your Mac types it.

Hold right ⌥ anywhere, say it, let go. The text lands in whatever app you’re in: Slack, Mail, Cursor, Notes, a terminal. Whisper runs on your Mac, so your voice never leaves it.

Download for Mac or install with Homebrew

Free and open source. macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon.

Languages

It never makes you pick a language.

Dictation usually asks which language you’ll speak, then stumbles over the other one. Here a Slack message that starts in Indonesian and ends in English comes out exactly that way. Nothing is translated, and meeting, deploy and merge stay in English because that’s how you said them.

Speed and privacy

0.5s

From letting go of the key to text in the field.

Whisper large-v3-turbo runs on the Apple Neural Engine and transcribes while you’re still talking, so when you release there’s almost nothing left to do. Measured on an M4. It used to take about three seconds.

The audio is processed on your Mac and stays there. So does your history.

Pro

Foxtation Pro writes down what you meant, not every eh.

On-device transcription keeps every um, every anu and every no wait. Pro sends the text, never the audio, to a cleanup model that removes the fillers, applies your self-corrections, fixes punctuation and spells your dictionary words the way you wrote them. The text isn’t stored.

Install

  1. Download the DMG and drag the app to Applications. Or use Homebrew:

    brew install --cask khmuhtadin/tap/foxtation

  2. The app isn’t notarized yet, so the first time, right-click it and choose Open. Or clear the quarantine flag:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Foxtation.app

  3. On first run it downloads the 1.5 GB speech model and prepares it. That takes about a minute, once.

Needs macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon.
